Notes:

- Filenames are case-sensitive on most web servers. Keep them lowercase exactly as above.
- If your photos are .png or .webp instead of .jpg, either rename them to .jpg,
  or open index.html and change the three `src="media/xxx-001.jpg"` lines to match
  your actual file extension.
- Until a photo is added, that spot on the site automatically shows a
  "Photography placeholder" label instead of a broken image — nothing breaks.
- Recommended: roughly 1000px+ on the shorter side, landscape or portrait both work
  since the site crops to fit (object-fit: cover).
- This file (media-readme.txt) is just a note for you — delete it any time,
  it is not linked from the site.
