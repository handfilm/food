Process

How we make it.

We won't publish the recipes. We will show you the system — the same discipline runs under all three products.

  1. 01

    Small batch

    Fixed batch sizes, planned before service, never scaled up mid-day.

  2. 02

    Controlled preparation

    Ingredients pre-portioned to weight and volume, not eyeballed.

  3. 03

    Fixed portions

    Every unit leaves the kitchen at the same size, every time.

  4. 04

    Fresh assembly

    Components are combined last, close to dispatch — not hours ahead.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Packed to hold texture and temperature until it reaches you.

Limited daily production

We don't manufacture more than we can control.

Batch sizes are fixed on purpose. When a day's batch is gone, it's gone — not as a marketing device, but because consistency only holds within a batch size we can actually supervise by hand.
